Trial credits are available as soon as you create an App. You call `https://api.trayce.africa/v1` as in production.

## Flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a test App">
    In the Dashboard, create an App with a clear label (e.g. `My project : Test`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Call the API">
    Use your key and realistic data to validate send / collect end to end.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor usage">
    Check the balance in the Dashboard (`app.trayce.africa`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go paid">
    When validated, activate the paid plan. App IDs and keys stay the same.
  </Step>
</Steps>
