## Overview

Trayce provides free trial credits when you create your App account. These credits let you test your integration in real conditions, without any billing.

## How it works

- Your trial credits are available immediately after creating an App in the Dashboard.
- Each operation (Collection created, document analyzed) consumes credits according to the pricing grid.
- Once credits are exhausted, switch to a paid plan from the Dashboard.

## Best practice

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a dedicated test App">
    Create a separate App in the Dashboard with a recognizable label (e.g., "My project — Test").
  </Step>

  <Step title="Use realistic test data">
    Trial credits work on the same endpoints as production. Use realistic data to validate your flows.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Monitor consumption">
    Check the Dashboard (`app.trayce.sn`) to track your credit usage.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go to production">
    Once your integration is validated, activate the paid plan. Your App IDs and API keys remain the same.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  There is no separate "Sandbox" environment. Trial credits are used on the same API as production — `https://api.trayce.africa/v1`.
</Note>
