Trayce connects organisations and people around trusted documents.

- **The Trayce app**, free, lets people keep their documents and proofs, and share them with their consent.
- **Trayce Business** lets organisations issue, request and verify documents.

The API gives access to Trayce Business from your own tools: HR software, property management, onboarding tools, access control.

## Capabilities

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Send" icon="send" href="/en/concepts/send">Issue signed documents that arrive in the person's app.</Card>
  <Card title="Collect" icon="inbox" href="/en/concepts/collect">Request documents. Each one arrives with its verification.</Card>
  <Card title="Verify" icon="badge-check" href="/en/concepts/verify">Verify a document received outside Trayce.</Card>
  <Card title="Connect" icon="user-check" href="/en/concepts/connect">Receive an identity already verified, with the person's consent.</Card>
  <Card title="Scan" icon="scan-line" href="/en/concepts/scan">Check on the spot the QR code shown by a person.</Card>
</CardGroup>
