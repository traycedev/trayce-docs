Trayce Business helps companies **send** and **collect** documents securely, then return a structured result.

| | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Send** | Deliver a document to a Trayce user. |
| **Collect** | Gather documents and information from one or more people. |

## Typical flow

```
App → Send or Collect → Workflow → Result → Webhook
```

<Note>
  Trayce returns the documentary result. The business decision stays with your company.
</Note>
