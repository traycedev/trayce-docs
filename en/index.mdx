## What is Trayce Business?

Trayce Business is a B2B platform that allows companies to manage the transmission, collection, and analysis of documents and information. The product is organised around two primitives: **send** and **collect**.

| Primitive | Purpose | Typical outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **send** | Send a document to a Trayce user. | The recipient receives a secure notification and can access the document in their Trayce account. |
| **collect** | Collect documents, information, and supporting evidence from one or more people. | A structured Collection is completed, analysed, and returned as a structured result. |

## From collection to decision

```
Company
  ↓
App
  ↓
send / collect
  ↓
Workflow
  ↓
Collection
  ↓
Persons / Documents / Requirements
  ↓
Trayce AI Engine
  ↓
Business Rules
  ↓
Scoring (optional)
  ↓
Structured Result
  ↓
Company Decision
```

<Note>
  Trayce provides documentary and analytical results. The final business decision remains the responsibility of the company.
</Note>
