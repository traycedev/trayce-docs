At an entrance, a desk or an event, the person shows a QR code from their Trayce app. You check it without ever seeing their documents.

## How it works

1. The person displays their QR code. It changes regularly and is valid for two minutes, once.
2. You scan it with the **Trayce Scan** app, or with your own tool through the API.
3. You get the result: valid or not, identity verified or not, over 18 or not, depending on what the person chose to share.

## What is guaranteed

- **No document shown**: only answers, for example "over 18: yes".
- **No copied QR codes**: a QR code already used or too old is refused.
- **Repeat visits flagged**: an earlier visit to you is flagged, useful to spot a lent QR code.

## Example: a festival entrance

An adults-only festival checks age at the entrance.

1. The visitor opens their app and displays their QR code.
2. The agent scans it with Trayce Scan, or the entrance gate calls [Check a QR code](/en/api-reference/scan/presentations).
3. The response says `age_over_18: true`: entry is granted. The agent sees neither the name, nor the date of birth, nor the ID document.
4. If the same QR code is shown a second time, it is refused (`already_used`).

## In the API

[Check a QR code](/en/api-reference/scan/presentations) is available to pilots.
