At an entrance, a desk or an event, the person shows their Trayce Pass, a QR code displayed in their app. You check it without ever seeing their documents.

## How it works

1. The person displays their Pass. It changes regularly and is valid for two minutes, once.
2. You scan it with the **Trayce Scan** app, or with your own tool through the API.
3. You get the result: valid or not, identity verified or not, over 18 or not, depending on what the person chose to share.

## What is guaranteed

- **No document shown**: only answers, for example "over 18: yes".
- **No copied Passes**: a Pass already used or too old is refused.
- **Repeat visits flagged**: an earlier visit to you is flagged, useful to spot a lent Pass.

## Example: a festival entrance

An adults-only festival checks age at the entrance.

1. The visitor opens their app and displays their Pass.
2. The agent scans it with Trayce Scan, or the entrance gate calls [Check a Pass](/en/api-reference/scan/presentations).
3. The response says `age_over_18: true`: entry is granted. The agent sees neither the name, nor the date of birth, nor the ID document.
4. If the same Pass is shown a second time, it is refused (`already_used`).

## In the API

[Check a Pass](/en/api-reference/scan/presentations) is available to pilots.
