## What collect does

**collect** creates a structured request for documents, information, personal data, and supporting evidence. A Collection may involve one person or several people.

## Collection lifecycle

```
DRAFT
  ↓
ACTIVE
  ↓
IN_PROGRESS
  ↓
SUBMITTED
  ↓
PROCESSING
  ↓
COMPLETED
```

Alternative states: `EXPIRED`, `CANCELLED`, `REQUIRES_ACTION`, `REVIEW_REQUIRED`, `FAILED`.

## Collection structure

```
COLLECTION
├── Workflow
├── Main Applicant
│   ├── Identity
│   ├── Documents
│   └── Data
├── Additional Persons
│   ├── Person 1
│   │   ├── Documents
│   │   └── Data
│   └── Person 2
│       ├── Documents
│       └── Data
├── Requirements
├── Analysis
├── Business Rules
├── Scoring
├── Result
└── Links
```

## Multi-person collection

A Collection is not limited to a single participant. Additional people can be added after the Collection is created. Each person can receive a dedicated access link, and their documents are associated with the correct participant within the same Collection.
