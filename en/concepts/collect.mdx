With Collect, you request documents from a person: ID document, payslips, proof of address.

## How it works

1. You create a request: the documents you need, a message, a deadline.
2. The person replies from their Trayce app, or through a link if they do not have Trayce yet.
3. The documents arrive in your workspace, **each with its verification**.

## Verify is included

Every document received is verified automatically, with no extra call:
- it is analysed (**N1**);
- if it was issued in Trayce, its signature is checked (**N2**);
- you can request source confirmation (**N3**) for the documents that matter.

See [verification levels](/en/concepts/verify).

## What is guaranteed

- The person chooses what they send and sees who to.
- Documents are encrypted for your organisation: Trayce cannot read them.

## Example: a rental application

A property agency asks Aïssatou Ba, who is applying for a rental, for the documents of her application.

1. The agency creates the request with [Request documents](/en/api-reference/collect/create):

```json
{
  "recipient_phone": "+221771234567",
  "documents": [
    { "type": "national_id" },
    { "type": "payslip", "count": 3 },
    { "type": "proof_of_address" }
  ],
  "message": "Documents for your rental application.",
  "external_id": "LOC-2026-118"
}
```

2. Aïssatou replies from her app in a few taps: her documents are already there.
3. The agency receives the `collect.submitted` event, then reads the [received documents](/en/api-reference/collect/documents): payslips issued by the employer in Trayce arrive at level **N2**, the proof of address at level **N1**.
4. For the payslips, the agency requests [source confirmation](/en/api-reference/verify/confirm): the employer confirms, and they move to level **N3**.
