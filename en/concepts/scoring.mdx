---

## Principle

Scoring is a **configurable business capability**, deliberately separated from the Trayce AI Engine.

```
Documents
  ↓
AI Engine
  ↓
Verified Data
  ↓
Business Rules / Scoring Engine
  ↓
Score + Explanation
```

## Example scoring profile

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Income | 40% |
| Employment Stability | 25% |
| Affordability | 20% |
| Document Consistency | 15% |

## Scoring result

A scoring result may include:
- A global score
- Scores by criterion
- The data used
- Applied rules
- Explanations

## Profile configuration

Enable scoring when creating the Collection (`configuration.scoring.enabled` + `profile_id`). The profile defines criteria, weights, and thresholds; the result includes the overall score, per-criterion detail, and an explanation.

## Responsibility

Trayce returns the score and explanation. The business decision (acceptance, rental, credit, etc.) stays with your company.
