Your customers may have already verified their identity in Trayce. With Connect, they share it with you in one step, with no new verification.

## How it works

1. You request an identity, in full or in part: full identity, over 18 or not, nationality.
2. The person sees in their app who is asking, for what and why.
3. They accept or decline.
4. If they accept, you only receive what they agreed to share.

## What is guaranteed

- **Nothing without consent**: every share is requested and accepted.
- **The minimum**: for an age check, you receive "over 18: yes", never the date of birth.
- **An identifier of your own**: the person has a different identifier for each organisation, which cannot be used to track them elsewhere.

## Example: opening an account

Khady Sarr opens an account with Banque Horizon from the bank's website.

1. She enters her phone number. The bank calls [Request an identity](/en/api-reference/connect/create) with `claims: ["identity"]` and the reason "Opening your account".
2. Khady sees in her app: "Banque Horizon would like to verify your identity", with what will be shared.
3. She accepts. The bank receives her identity, already verified by Trayce, and opens the account without asking her again for a photo of her ID or a selfie.
