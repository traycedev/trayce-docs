Verify tells what a document is worth. It is included in [Collect](/en/concepts/collect), and also works on its own for documents received outside Trayce.

## Verification levels

Every verified document gets a level, which tells how far the verification went.

| Level | Name | What was verified |
|---|---|---|
| **N1** | Analysed | Trayce analysed the document with artificial intelligence: consistency, readability, signs of tampering. |
| **N2** | Signature verified | The document was issued in Trayce by a verified organisation. Its signature is valid and it has not been changed since. |
| **N3** | Confirmed at source | The issuer of the document, or an official registry, confirmed it is authentic. |

A document issued with [Send](/en/concepts/send) arrives at level **N2**. The level informs you, the decision stays yours.

<Note>Organisations that issue in Trayce are verified by the Trayce team before they can issue. This is what gives level N2 its value.</Note>

## Two ways to use it

- **With Collect**: every document received comes with its verification, with no extra call.
- **On its own**: you import a document received elsewhere (email, desk, paper). Trayce analyses it (**N1**). If it was issued in Trayce, its signature is checked (**N2**).

## Confirm at source

For an important document, request confirmation from its issuer or an official registry (**N3**). This works for a document received through Collect as well as an imported one. The answer may take time: the `verify.completed` event lets you know.

To analyse an imported document, Trayce has to read it. Documents received through Collect stay end-to-end encrypted.

## Example: a statement received by email

Banque Horizon receives by email the bank statement of a customer applying for a loan.

1. The advisor imports it with [Verify a document](/en/api-reference/verify/create). The analysis finds no sign of tampering: level **N1**.
2. The loan amount is high: the bank requests [source confirmation](/en/api-reference/verify/confirm).
3. The issuing institution confirms the statement. The bank receives `verify.completed`, and the document moves to level **N3**.
