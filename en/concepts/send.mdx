With Send, your organisation issues signed documents: payslips, certificates, contracts, invoices, statements.

## How it works

1. You send a document to the person's phone number.
2. It arrives in their Trayce app, already filed, at level **N2**: signature verified (see [levels](/en/concepts/verify)).
3. If they do not have Trayce yet, they receive a link to claim it, then the app.
4. You follow each step: delivered, opened, accepted or declined.

## What is guaranteed

- **Signed**: the person, and any organisation they show it to, can check it really comes from you and has not been changed.
- **End-to-end encrypted**: Trayce carries the document without being able to read it.
- **Revocable**: you can withdraw a document sent by mistake.

## Example: payslips

Teranga Services issues its 120 employees' payslips every month from its payroll software.

1. When payroll closes, the software calls [Send a document](/en/api-reference/send/create) for each employee, with `doc_type: "payslip"` and the payroll number as `external_id`.
2. Moussa Fall receives his payslip in his Trayce app, filed under **Work**.
3. The following month, he applies for a loan: he shares his last three payslips with his bank, which receives them at level N2, with nothing to check by hand.
4. HR sees in its tracking who opened and accepted their payslip.

## In the API

- [Send a document](/en/api-reference/send/create): available to pilots in test mode.
- Encrypted upload and live sending through the API open with the SDK. Until then, pilots send from their Trayce Business workspace.
