## End-to-end encryption

Documents issued with Send and documents received with Collect are encrypted before they leave their sender. Trayce carries and stores them without being able to read them. Only Verify, when you import a document received elsewhere, asks Trayce to read it in order to analyse it.

## Nothing without consent

A person chooses what they send, what they share and with whom. For an age check, the organisation receives "over 18: yes", never the date of birth.

## An identifier for each organisation

With Connect and Scan, a person has a different identifier for each organisation. Two organisations cannot cross-reference their data to track them.

## Verified issuers

Every organisation is verified by the Trayce team before it can issue. This is what guarantees that a level N2 document really comes from its issuer.

## Your API keys

Keep them server-side, give each one only the permissions it needs, and revoke an exposed key right away. See [Authentication](/en/authentication).
