Trayce Business is your organisation's web workspace. It gives access to the same capabilities as the API, with no development:

- **Send**: issue documents to your customers, students or employees.
- **Collect**: request documents and receive them already verified.
- **Verify**: verify a document received outside Trayce.
- **Connect**: receive your customers' verified identity, with their consent.
- **Scan**: prepare your on-site checks, done with the Trayce Scan app.
- **API keys**: create and revoke your organisation's keys.
- **Team**: invite your colleagues and choose their rights.

Trayce Business workspaces are opened by the Trayce team, after a demo. [Request a demo](https://trayce.africa/en).
