## Access

The Trayce Dashboard is available at [app.trayce.sn](https://app.trayce.sn).

## Features

### Apps

Create and manage your Apps. Each App has its own API keys and configuration settings. You can create multiple Apps to separate environments or projects.

### Collections

View all your Collections, their status, and analysis results. Filter by workflow, status, or date.

### Human review

Process Collections in `REVIEW_REQUIRED` status. View submitted documents, extracted data, and make an acceptance or rejection decision.

### Webhooks

Configure your webhook endpoints and select which events to receive. View delivery history.

### Credits

Track your trial credit consumption and manage your plan.

### Settings

- Organization information
- Team members and permissions
- Billing

## Dashboard roles

| Role | Access |
|---|---|
| `OWNER` | Full access, including billing and account deletion |
| `ADMIN` | Full access except billing |
| `OPERATOR` | View and human review |
| `DEVELOPER` | Technical access (API keys, webhooks) |

<Note>
  Fine-grained role and permission management is being finalized.
