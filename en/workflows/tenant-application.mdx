## Purpose

Collect, verify, and analyze a tenant rental application.

## Requirements by participant

| Participant | Requirement | Required |
|---|---|---|
| `PRIMARY_APPLICANT` | Identity document | Yes |
| `PRIMARY_APPLICANT` | Proof of address | Yes |
| `PRIMARY_APPLICANT` | Employment certificate | Yes |
| `PRIMARY_APPLICANT` | Pay slips | 3 required |
| `PRIMARY_APPLICANT` | Bank statements | Optional |
| `CO_TENANT` | Identity | Per workflow |
| `CO_TENANT` | Pay slips | e.g., 3 |
| `CO_TENANT` | Proof of address | Per workflow |

## Available checks

- Identity, employer, address, dates
- Income
- Cross-document consistency
- Authenticity, anomalies, document fraud detection
- Scoring and business rules
