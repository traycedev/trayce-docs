## Purpose

Collect and verify information about a company as part of a B2B relationship.

## Collected documents

- Registration documents (NINEA, RCCM, Kbis…)
- Tax documents
- Legal documents (articles of association…)
- Identity document of the legal representative
- Ultimate Beneficial Owner (UBO) information
- Registered office proof
- Additional configured documents

## Available checks

- Document extraction and verification
- Company / representative consistency
- UBO rules (if configured and available)
- Configured business rules

## Participants

This workflow can involve multiple persons (legal representative, UBOs…).

## Workflow code

```
KYB
```
