## Test mode

`trc_test_` keys are free, with no time limit. Calls are validated as in live mode, but no document is sent, no person is contacted and nothing is billed.

## Going live

`trc_live_` keys take real effect. They are available once the Trayce team has opened your Trayce Business workspace. During the pilot programme, access is early, with trial credits.

If your trial ends, live calls are refused with the `trial_expired` error. Your keys and settings stay in place: [contact us](/en/support) to continue.
