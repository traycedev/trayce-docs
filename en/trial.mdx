## Why there is no Sandbox

The Trayce Business product model is based on a **trial period with free credits** rather than separate Sandbox and Production environments. The trial lets you integrate and test under the same conditions as production, with no additional infrastructure.

## Trial period

The trial allows a company to evaluate Trayce and validate its integration before moving to paid usage. Trial access is based on free credits.

## Free credits

Credits represent the usage allowance offered during the trial.

<Note>
  The exact number of credits, expiration rules, and consumption rates are commercial parameters that have not yet been defined. This page will be updated before publication.
</Note>

| Topic | Description |
|---|---|
| Eligibility | Any new company creating a Trayce Business account. |
| Initial allocation | To be defined. |
| Expiration | To be defined. |
| Consumption | Credit-consuming operations will be listed here. |
| Balance | Visible from the Dashboard, App tab. |
| Exhaustion | When the balance reaches zero, credit-gated operations are blocked until upgrade. |
| Upgrade | Move to the paid offering from the Dashboard. |

## Monitoring usage during the trial

The Dashboard displays in real time:
- Remaining credit balance
- Breakdown of consumed operations
- Trial expiry date (if applicable)

## Moving to paid usage

<Note>
  Billing rules and pricing will be published once the pricing model is finalised.
</Note>

Once free credits are exhausted or the trial period ends, the company can move to the paid offering from the Dashboard. No technical migration is required — existing Apps, API keys, and webhooks remain operational.
