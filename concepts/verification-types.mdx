Trayce supports four core verification types, each designed for a distinct compliance or operational use case. All types share the same underlying request lifecycle — you initiate the check via the API or Business Portal, the vault holder consents, and Trayce returns a structured result. What differs is the document category, the checks performed, and the result fields returned.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="KYC">
    ## KYC — Know Your Customer

    KYC verification confirms the real-world identity of an individual using a government-issued identity document combined with a biometric liveness check. Trayce extracts and validates data from the document, compares it against the liveness capture, and checks the document's authenticity markers.

    **What Trayce checks:**
    - Document authenticity (chip data, security features, expiry)
    - Face match between the document photo and the liveness capture
    - Name, date of birth, nationality, and document number extraction
    - Sanctions and PEP (Politically Exposed Person) screening (available as an add-on)

    **Supported documents:**
    - National identity card
    - Passport
    - Driver's license (where accepted as a primary ID)

    **Common use cases:**
    - Customer onboarding for financial products
    - AML (Anti-Money Laundering) compliance
    - Age verification
    - Remote account opening for banks, fintechs, and exchanges
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="KYB">
    ## KYB — Know Your Business

    KYB verification confirms the legal existence of a company and validates its registration details, directors, and beneficial owners. Trayce cross-references submitted documents against official company registries where available, and returns a structured result covering the entity's standing.

    **What Trayce checks:**
    - Company registration number and legal name
    - Registered address and jurisdiction
    - Director and beneficial owner identities (linked to KYC checks where required)
    - Company status (active, dissolved, suspended)

    **Supported documents:**
    - Articles of incorporation / certificate of incorporation
    - Company registry extract
    - Beneficial ownership declaration
    - VAT or tax registration certificate

    **Common use cases:**
    - B2B onboarding and supplier verification
    - Corporate account opening
    - Grant and tender eligibility checks
    - Marketplace seller verification
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Diploma Verification">
    ## Diploma Verification

    Diploma verification confirms the authenticity of academic and professional credentials. Trayce validates the document's format and security features, and where supported, cross-references with issuing institution records or accreditation databases.

    **What Trayce checks:**
    - Document authenticity and institution details
    - Degree type, field of study, and graduation date
    - Holder name and match against identity documents (optional)
    - Accreditation status of the issuing institution (where available)

    **Supported credential types:**
    - Bachelor's degree
    - Master's degree
    - Doctoral (PhD) certificate
    - Professional certifications and trade qualifications
    - Continuing education certificates

    **Common use cases:**
    - HR background checks and pre-hire credential screening
    - Education platform enrollment verification
    - Regulated profession licensing (medical, legal, engineering)
    - Scholarship and grant eligibility
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Civil Status">
    ## Civil Status Verification

    Civil status verification validates official civil registry documents — birth certificates, marriage certificates, family records, and related documents. These checks confirm the document's authenticity and extract structured data for downstream use in legal, insurance, or government workflows.

    **What Trayce checks:**
    - Document authenticity and issuing authority
    - Key data fields (names, dates, registration numbers)
    - Document integrity (unaltered, unexpired where applicable)

    **Supported document types:**
    - Birth certificate
    - Marriage certificate
    - Divorce decree
    - Death certificate
    - Family record book (livret de famille)

    **Common use cases:**
    - Insurance policy underwriting and claims processing
    - Legal services and notarial processes
    - Government portal identity verification
    - Inheritance and estate administration
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Verification Result Fields

All verification types return a consistent result envelope. The `details` object contains verification-type-specific fields in addition to the standard fields below.

| Field | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `status` | `string` | Lifecycle state of the verification request. One of `pending`, `in_progress`, `completed`, or `failed`. |
| `result` | `string` | Outcome of the completed verification. One of `approved`, `rejected`, or `manual_review`. |
| `verified_at` | `string (ISO 8601)` | Timestamp of when the verification was completed. `null` if not yet completed. |
| `details` | `object` | Verification-type-specific fields, such as extracted document data, matched fields, and sub-check results. See the API reference for each type's `details` schema. |

<Tip>
  You can combine multiple verification types in a single workflow by chaining requests or using Trayce's **campaign** feature in the Business Portal. For example, a financial services onboarding flow can trigger a KYC check and a proof-of-address check simultaneously, requiring only one interaction from the end user.
</Tip>
