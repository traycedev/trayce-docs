Trayce is a secure document management and identity verification platform that connects individuals, businesses, and their documents through a privacy-first architecture. Whether you're onboarding new customers, verifying business partners, or collecting certified credentials, Trayce provides a unified layer that handles consent, encryption, and verification — so you don't have to.

## Core Components

The Trayce platform is built on three interconnected layers, each designed for a distinct audience and purpose.

## Verification Lifecycle

Every verification on Trayce follows a consistent, auditable lifecycle regardless of the verification type or integration method.

1. **Request** — Your platform (or the Business Portal) initiates a verification request, specifying the type of check required and the target user.
2. **Notification** — Trayce notifies the vault holder via push notification or email, prompting them to review the request.
3. **Submission** — The user opens their Vault, reviews the request, and explicitly approves sharing the relevant document or credential.
4. **Review** — Trayce performs automated checks (liveness, document authenticity, data extraction). Some verification types may involve a human review step for edge cases.
5. **Result** — The outcome is delivered to your platform via webhook and made available through the API. Results include a status, result code, and a structured `details` object.

## Data Privacy

Trayce is built on a consent-first data model. Documents stored in a user's Vault are encrypted at rest using keys that only the user controls. Businesses never receive raw document data unless the vault holder explicitly consents to share it.

In most verification flows, your platform receives only the **verification result** — a structured response confirming whether the check passed, failed, or requires further review. The underlying document never leaves the user's device unless consent is granted.

When a user does consent to document sharing, the transmission is end-to-end encrypted and scoped strictly to the request. Trayce does not retain copies of shared documents beyond the configurable retention window.

<Note>
  Trayce is fully GDPR-compliant. All personal data is processed and stored within the European Union by default. Enterprise customers can configure data residency settings to meet regional regulatory requirements. Audit logs are immutable and available for compliance reporting.
</Note>
