Trayce is an identity verification and secure document exchange platform designed for businesses that operate in regulated industries, onboard customers remotely, or need a reliable way to collect and validate sensitive documents. From a single REST API, you can trigger KYC and KYB checks, verify academic and civil status records, and exchange confidential files with individuals — all with end-to-end encryption, consent tracking, and a full audit trail built in. Trayce is built for compliance teams, product engineers, and operations teams who need verification workflows that are both developer-friendly and regulation-ready.

## What You Can Do With Trayce

Trayce consolidates several verification and document workflows into one platform:

- **KYC (Know Your Customer) Verification** — Verify the identity of individual users using government-issued identity documents (passports, national IDs, driver's licences), selfie liveness checks, and watchlist screening.
- **KYB (Know Your Business) Verification** — Validate the legitimacy of business entities by checking company registration certificates, beneficial ownership declarations, and corporate structure documents.
- **Diploma & Academic Credential Verification** — Confirm the authenticity of university degrees, diplomas, and professional certifications against institutional records.
- **Civil Status Document Verification** — Verify birth certificates, marriage certificates, and other civil registry documents for compliance and due diligence workflows.
- **Document Requests** — Programmatically request specific documents from an individual. The request is delivered to their Trayce Vault app, where they can approve and share with a single tap.
- **Secure Document Sending** — Push encrypted documents to an individual's Vault app, ensuring delivery, consent acknowledgement, and tamper-proof storage.

## How Trayce Works

Trayce is built around three interconnected layers that work together to make verification and document exchange secure, auditable, and privacy-respecting.

### 1. The Trayce Vault App (Individual Layer)

The Trayce Vault is a mobile application for individuals. It acts as a secure, encrypted personal document store where users upload, manage, and share their identity and credential documents. When your business sends a document request via the API, the individual receives a notification in their Vault app. They review the request and explicitly grant or deny access — Trayce never shares a document without the individual's active consent. All documents in the Vault are encrypted at rest and in transit.

### 2. The Business Portal (Operations Layer)

The Business Portal is the web dashboard available to your team at [app.trayce.io](https://app.trayce.io). Use it to manage verification cases, review flagged submissions, configure webhook endpoints, generate and rotate API keys, and monitor your organisation's verification activity. The Portal is designed for compliance officers, operations teams, and account managers who need visibility without writing code.

### 3. The Trayce API (Integration Layer)

The Trayce REST API is how your product communicates with the platform programmatically. All verification requests, document exchanges, status queries, and webhook configurations are available through the API. Every API call is authenticated, every action is logged, and every response is consistently structured so you can build reliable, predictable integrations.

## Base URLs

Trayce provides separate environments for development and production. Use the sandbox URL while building and testing your integration, and the production URL when you go live.

| Environment | Base URL |
|---|---|
| **Production** | `https://api.trayce.io/v1` |
| **Sandbox** | `https://sandbox.api.trayce.io/v1` |

Append the relevant endpoint path to the appropriate base URL for every request. For example, to create a verification in the sandbox: `POST https://sandbox.api.trayce.io/v1/verifications`.

<Note>
  All calls to the Trayce API must include a valid `Authorization: Bearer <token>` header. Unauthenticated requests will receive a `401 Unauthorized` response. See the [Authentication](/authentication) page for full details on API keys and OAuth 2.0.
</Note>
